04WHAT TO NOTICE

Decision map

  1. ✓
    ClassificationCompare the prediction with the provisional label.
  2. ◇
    RefusalKeep the row; do not turn it into a negative label.
  3. !
    Parse failureRecord why the response cannot be scored.
  4. ?
    Label disagreementReview the code and rationale; the label may be wrong.

PROVISIONAL LABEL = working reference. Stored status: PROVISIONAL_GROUND_TRUTH.

ENGINEER’S LENS

Metrics are only as reliable as their labels. Review disagreements before treating the reference as settled truth.
