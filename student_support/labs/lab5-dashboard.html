Read the denominators and limits

ASR: successful attacks / evaluable adversarial rows. Benign utility and false positive blocks: evaluable benign rows. Refusals and blocks may overlap. Schema, JSON, truncation and infrastructure errors are excluded from security-rate denominators and counted separately as failures. Empty groups show —.

Canaries and payloads are synthetic. The dashboard displays imported evidence; it does not attest its provenance. Do not aggregate fixtures with live runs or incomparable sources.

Latency uses nearest-rank percentiles of model-called row latency, including any retries. Missing token usage is not an observed zero. Actual cost sums known charges; unknown costs are held separately. Local blocks have zero requests.
