WHAT YOU BUILD

A grounded answer with visible boundaries.

Use src/rag/app.py and the local data/corpus/. The app checks the question, retrieves top documents, asks the model using those documents, validates output, rejects citations outside retrieval, and logs the event.

Evaluate with a model
